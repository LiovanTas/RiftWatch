How the score works: each part is measured from stored ranked games and pulled towards "no effect" when the games are few, then the parts are added on the log-odds scale. Team-comp parts are small on purpose: RiftWatch's win-probability model finds that a draft alone barely predicts a solo-queue game, and the damage-mix and frontline effects come from how teams with each mix have actually done. Your comfort and playstyle need your Riot ID and stored games (search yourself first).
