How a review works

the method →
01

Every game, laid out

Items, runes, KDA, farm, vision and all ten players for each game, with build order, skill order and the gold lead over time.

02

Scored against your rank

Each minute is compared with players at your rank, in your role, on your champion and matchup, so the numbers mean something.

03

Coaching that cites its sources

An AI coach explains the biggest gaps, and every number it quotes has to appear in the measured evidence.

04

Habits over time

Weekly progress against a fixed yardstick, your champion pool, and whether you slip late in a session or after losses.

05

Learned from high-elo play

Models trained on Master+ games and replays show what top players do in spots like yours.

06

Live and meta

Scout everyone in a game in progress, and check the tier board and builds from {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.ranked) }} ranked games.
