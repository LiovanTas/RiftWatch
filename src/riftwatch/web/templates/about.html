How RiftWatch works

Most stat sites show you what happened. RiftWatch also tells you how it compares with players at your rank, and why.

{{ icon('history') }}
Match history from Riot

Search a Riot ID and RiftWatch downloads your ranked and draft games from Riot's API, with each game's minute-by-minute timeline. Games never change once played, so each is downloaded once and kept.

{{ icon('chart') }}
Scored against your rank, in your role

Every minute of your game (farm, gold, experience, vision, the gap to your lane opponent) is compared with players at your rank in the same role, adjusted for champion and matchup. "Better than 70%" means you beat 70% of comparable players on that stat.

{{ icon('brain') }}
An AI coach that can't make things up

The coach only sees computed evidence: each point it makes must cite that evidence, and a checker removes any point whose numbers don't appear in what it cites.

{{ icon('eye') }}
Models trained on high-elo play

Decision models learn from thousands of Master+ games what top players do in each situation, and a laning model learns from high-elo replays which trades they take. Each model is scored on games it never saw and only used where it beats a plain baseline.

{{ icon('trophy') }}
Champion statistics

Tier lists and builds come from the {{ '{:,}'.format(ranked) }} ranked games RiftWatch has analysed. Win rates are shrunk towards 50% for small samples, so a champion that went 3-0 isn't called a 100% pick.
