RiftWatch metric

Swing

How much each player moved their team's chance of winning, in percentage points. A neural network reads the state of a game at any moment and gives each team's chance to win. Every change in that chance, from the draft to the end, is shared out among the players responsible, and each moment says why it moved the chance as much as it did. Counting the draft, the winners' Swing always adds up to +50 and the losers' to -50; a player's Swing is their part of it.

01The networktested on {{ '{:,}'.format(m.test_games) }} games it never saw

A small neural network (two hidden layers of {{ m.network.layers|join(' and ') }} units) trained on {{ '{:,}'.format(m.games) }} ranked games, every minute from both teams' sides ({{ '{:,}'.format(m.rows) }} moments). It reads the game state: the minute; the gold, XP, CS and level gaps; kills; towers; inhibitors currently down; dragons and soul; Baron and Elder (taken, and whether the buff is still up); heralds, voidgrubs and Atakhan; and how many players on each side are alive right now.{% if m.chosen == 'stacked' %} A logistic layer on top adds each team's finished items by what they give ({{ m.items_known }} items: armor, magic resist, health, damage, crit, penetration, lifesteal, anti-heal), how they match the enemy's damage (armor against physical, magic resist against magic), the teams' classes, and each champion's strength and how it changes over the game.{% endif %} It's then calibrated on separate games, so "70%" means that team won about 70% of the time.

{{ '04' if m.calibration else '03' }}How it's shared out
{% if m.chosen == 'stacked' %}
DraftThe network knows the champions, so a game can start above or below 50%: the difference is the two drafts, shared by each team.
{% endif %}
KillsA kill's jump goes half to the killer and half to the assisters (all to the killer if nobody helped).
DeathsThe player who died takes the other team's gain, and half of whatever their team loses before they respawn.
ObjectivesDragons, Baron, Elder, heralds, voidgrubs and Atakhan: half the gain goes to the players who took part, half to the whole team (who made it possible); the other team shares the loss.
StructuresTowers and inhibitors, the same way.
{% if m.chosen == 'stacked' %}
ItemsFinishing an item moves the chance by what it gives against this enemy team; the buyer gets it.
{% endif %}
EconomyWhatever moved between fights (farming, plates, trading, pressure) goes to the team it favoured, half evenly and half by gold and XP earned that minute; the other team shares the loss.
Why a moment cost what it didEach jump is split exactly among what changed (a player down, the bounty, the kill score, the dragon, an item's armor) by Shapley values: each change's average effect over every order the changes could be added in. Deaths also record the damage, place, timer, gold held and what the enemy took meanwhile.

Choices: items, skills, runes, spells{{ '{:,}'.format(choice_summary.choices) }} choices in stored games

Each choice is compared with the win chance the network gave the player's team when it was made: the item when it was finished, the first ability maxed at 3 minutes, the keystone and spells at the start. A raw win rate by item mostly measures who was already winning; this takes the game state out. Averages are pulled towards zero by how much real difference there is between choices (empirical Bayes), and items are also measured against the enemy team's damage type and tanks.
