RiftWatch metric

Swing

How much each player moved their team's chance of winning, in percentage points. A neural network reads the state of a game at any moment and gives each team's chance to win. Every change in that chance, from the first minute to the end, is shared out among the players responsible. Every game starts at 50%, so the winners' Swing adds up to +50 and the losers' to -50; a player's Swing is their part of it.

A small neural network (two hidden layers of {{ m.network.layers|join(' and ') }} units) trained on {{ '{:,}'.format(m.games) }} ranked games, every minute from both teams' sides ({{ '{:,}'.format(m.rows) }} moments). It reads {{ m.features|length }} numbers: the minute; the gold, XP, CS and level gaps; kills; towers; inhibitors currently down; dragons and soul; Baron and Elder (taken, and whether the buff is still up); heralds, voidgrubs and Atakhan; and how many players on each side are alive right now. It's then calibrated on separate games, so "70%" means that team won about 70% of the time.

{{ '04' if m.calibration else '03' }}How it's shared out
FightsA kill's jump goes half to the killer and half to the assisters (all to the killer if nobody helped). The player who died takes the other team's loss.
ObjectivesDragons, Baron, Elder, heralds, voidgrubs and Atakhan: half the gain goes to the players who took part, half to the whole team (who made it possible); the other team shares the loss.
StructuresTowers and inhibitors, the same way.
EconomyWhatever moved between fights (farming, plates, trading, pressure) goes to the team it favoured, half evenly and half by gold and XP earned that minute; the other team shares the loss.
